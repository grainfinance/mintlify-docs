This guide walks you through integrating the Grain API — from retrieving your credentials to booking your first hedge in Sandbox.

**New to FX hedging?** Start with [Core Concepts](/concepts/core-concepts) to understand exposure, hedges, quotes, and settlement before you write any code. It only takes a few minutes and the steps below will make much more sense.

## How onboarding works

Getting started with Grain happens in two phases:

1. **Test in Sandbox.** Start in the Sandbox environment with a light setup and book your first hedge through the dashboard and the API.
2. **Go live.** When you're ready for real transactions, complete your full configuration with your Grain contact, and Grain activates your Live environment. The steps below walk through the Sandbox phase.
