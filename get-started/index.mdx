The Grain platform lets you lock cross-currency rates into the future and protect from cross-currency risk. This guide walks you through your first API integration.

**New to FX hedging?** Start with [Core Concepts](https://docs.grainfinance.co/concepts/core-concepts) to understand exposure, hedges, quotes, and settlement before you write any code. It only takes a few minutes and the steps below will make much more sense.

## How onboarding works

Getting started with Grain happens in two phases:

1. **Test in Sandbox.** You begin in the Sandbox environment with a setup scoped to what you want to test — at a minimum, one currency pair, a wallet, and a settlement type, which is enough to create a customer and book your first hedge through the dashboard and the API.
2. **Go live.** When you're ready for real transactions, you complete your full configuration with your Grain contact and Grain activates your Live environment. The steps below walk through the Sandbox phase.
